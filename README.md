## `> whoami`

I take ideas from zero to production — Next.js frontends, NestJS APIs, Prisma/PostgreSQL data layers, real-time Socket.IO systems, and the occasional browser game. Most of what I build comes out of communities I'm part of: Toastie started as tooling for my Toastmasters club and grew into a full club operating system.
